# seize the future. for all.

A vertical (9:16) found-footage edit for TikTok and Reels, cut to **"i feel weird" by meat computer** (2:16, released Sept 2 2026).
It is corecore and political on purpose: against end-times fascism, the race to superintelligence and the concentration of wealth and power in a few hands, and a call for our generation to take the future back. We are nationalists of one nation: the nation of earth.
The symbol is the **One World Flag**, a blue dot on a transparent field.

## building it

```
./make.sh            # the edit: out/seize-the-future.mp4 (about 5 minutes; FAST=1 for a quicker, bigger encode)
./make.sh --draft    # the same picture over a temporary score, if the song isn't there
```

It needs the song at `music/i-feel-weird.mp3`, the news clips in `footage/news/` (both fetched by `tools/fetch_wanted.py`,
which needs Python 3.10+ and [deno](https://deno.com) for yt-dlp to reach YouTube),
and the Creative Commons footage and satellite frames (`tools/yfcc/fetch_picks.py` and `tools/sat/*.py` download them).
Setup is `apt install ffmpeg` and `pip install numpy scipy opencv-python-headless pillow librosa rasterio pyproj h5py`.

Every cut sits on the song's beat grid (141 BPM). Shot lengths are counted in beats, and each section starts where the song's does:
the bass and hi-hats enter at 13.70 s, the drop hits at 54.57, the breakdown at 81.80, the build at 95.42, the last chorus
at 109.04, and the music stops at 134.56. `edit/beats.py` prints a rough map of any song. When someone speaks, their voice
is evened out and the music steps back about 9 dB under it.

## what's here

| path | what |
|---|---|
| `edit/timeline.py` | the edit, shot by shot, and every word on screen |
| `edit/render.py` | the renderer (1080x1920, 30 fps) |
| `edit/audio.py` | the soundtrack: the song, the voices, the footage's own sound |
| `edit/wanted.json`, `edit/WANTED.md` | the news clips, where each comes from, and a prompt for an agent to fetch them |
| `tools/fetch_wanted.py` | fetches the song and those clips (yt-dlp; finds each moment in the captions) |
| `brand/MANIFESTO.md` | the poem, the symbol, the lines |
| `brand/symbol/` | the flag and the dot as SVG and transparent PNG, plus avatars |
| `brand/posters.py` | the edit as a carousel ("transmissions") plus printables: an A4 dot poster and a sheet of dots to cut out |
| `brand/fonts/` | Instrument Serif (words), IBM Plex Mono (facts), Inter (all OFL-licensed) |
| `edit/picks.tsv` | 154 Creative Commons clips chosen from YFCC100M, with notes |
| `edit/credits_yfcc.tsv` | author, title, licence and source for every one of them |
| `tools/yfcc/` | how the footage was found: a scanner that streamed YFCC100M's 65 GB metadata into a searchable index, then contact sheets |
| `tools/sat/` | satellite imagery: NOAA GOES-19 full-disk Earth, ESA Sentinel-2 true colour |
| `CREDITS.md` | everything in the cut, in order (regenerated by `tools/credits.py`) |

## where everything comes from

- **News clips:** each one's source, channel and date is saved next to it in `footage/news/<id>.json`, and listed in
  `CREDITS.md`. They're quoted for commentary and criticism and still belong to their owners. Dialogue is subtitled
  word for word from the clips. The Thiel clip is a repost of NYT Opinion's own vertical cut and keeps the NYT captions.
  The repost's TikTok watermark is blurred, because Instagram pushes down reels that carry one.
- **Footage:** YFCC100M via the Multimedia Commons (AWS Open Data), Flickr videos from 2004 to 2014 shared under Creative Commons.
  Only Attribution, Attribution-ShareAlike and NonCommercial variants are used. NoDerivatives licences are excluded because they forbid remixing.
  Credit them (a link in bio works). Because some clips are ShareAlike or NonCommercial, keep the piece non-commercial and share it under CC BY-NC-SA.
- **Earth:** NOAA GOES-19 ABI, full disk, Sept 29 2026 (US government work, public domain).
- **Ground:** contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026 (ESA): Washington DC on Sept 16, Abilene TX on Sept 15.
- **Symbol:** the One World Flag by Thomas Mandl (2016), public domain.
- **Words on screen, verified before use:**
  - The richest 1% own 43.8% of the world's wealth, and the poorer half own 0.52% (Oxfam, *Resisting the Rule of the Rich*, Jan 19 2026).
  - Doomsday Clock set to 85 seconds to midnight on Jan 27 2026, the closest ever (Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists).
  - "SI instead of AI… you know what that means? super intelligent.": President Trump at the White House on Sept 29 2026,
    after signing an order that federal agencies call AI "Super Intelligence" (Fox News feed; CNBC, whitehouse.gov).
